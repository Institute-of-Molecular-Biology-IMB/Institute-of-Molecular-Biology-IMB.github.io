# Institute of Molecular Biology (IMB) — Official Website

This repository hosts the official public portal for the **Institute of Molecular Biology (IMB)**:
Website: [https://institute-of-molecular-biology-imb.github.io/](https://institute-of-molecular-biology-imb.github.io/)

## Core Research Areas
- Computational Structural Biology and Molecular Docking (AlphaFold2, AutoDock Vina, STN7 and STN8 chloroplast kinases)
- Wheat Heat Stress and Membrane Damage Modeling (LT50 non-linear sigmoidal regressions, MDR, HSP16.9)
- Systems Oncology and Machine Learning (TNBC tumor microenvironment, WGCNA, co-expression networks)
- Smart Urban Agriculture and Environmental Modeling (InvestArea project, 1st Place Karabakh Hackathon 2026)
